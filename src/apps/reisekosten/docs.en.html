Travel expenses – how it works

Capture a trip with as little input as possible — destination, departure and return plus the meals provided are enough. The app automatically derives the meal allowances (with arrival/departure logic), the meal reductions, the mileage and the accommodation, and shows the total. All trips stay local in your browser — nothing is uploaded.

Meal allowance (per diem)

  • Domestic (Germany): full day (24 hrs) €28, arrival/departure day or a single day over 8 hrs €14. A single day up to 8 hrs earns no allowance.
  • Abroad: country-specific full daily rate from the BMF table; the arrival/departure day (and > 8 hrs) counts at 80 % of it.
  • Meal reduction per provided meal, taken from the full daily rate: breakfast −20 %, lunch −40 %, dinner −40 % (domestic: −€5.60 / −€11.20 / −€11.20). A day never goes negative.

Cost table (travel, accommodation, incidentals)

All costs live in one table. Each row has a category (travel, accommodation or incidentals), a description and an amount. Use + Travel, + Accommodation or + Incidental to add rows manually; receipts create a new position automatically (see below).

  • Travel: either kilometres × rate (car €0.30/km, motorcycle €0.20/km) or a fixed amount (train, flight, public transport per receipt).
  • Accommodation: either nights × flat rate (domestic €20, abroad country-specific) or the actual receipted amount. Nights are pre-filled from the period and editable. If breakfast is included, mark the next day's breakfast as “provided”.
  • Incidentals (parking, tolls, luggage, city/accommodation tax …) as their own positions — so you can capture e.g. an accommodation tax in addition to the hotel cost.

Meal allowances remain separate under Days & meals.

Import receipts (PDF & photo)

In the Costs section you can import a receipt as a PDF, photo or scan — by click, by drag & drop, and several files at once. The app reads the text (directly for PDFs, via OCR for photos/scans) and automatically detects the amount, date/period and receipt type — for hotel invoices also an included breakfast and the number of nights, for train/transit tickets the outbound/return and route.

With several receipts a selection list appears (type · amount · period). You tick which to add as a new position; if periods differ (e.g. a stray receipt) that is flagged so you can deselect it. Optionally the trip period or breakfast can be set from the receipts. Your manual entries stay in charge and the receipt serves as a check. The imported receipt is saved with the trip and can be reopened any time via “View” (image or PDF), downloaded or removed. Everything runs locally in your browser; the receipt is not uploaded. The reader library is loaded once from the network on the first import (available afterwards). Photos are downscaled for storage; very large files are not embedded (the detected data is kept).

Settlement & export

  • Print / PDF: produces a clean travel-expense settlement sheet with a per-day breakdown and signature lines — save it as PDF from the browser's print dialog.
  • CSV: export a single trip or all trips for accounting/Excel.
  • Export with data (footer) saves a snapshot of all trips into a standalone HTML file; reopening it restores the data.

Settings

The domestic amounts and mileage rates can be adjusted in Settings, as can the perspective (employee → employer or self-employed / tax return). For trips abroad every rate is editable per trip, and “Other country / custom rates…” lets you capture any destination with your own amounts.

Note: all amounts are guideline values without warranty. Meal and accommodation flat rates are annual values (BMF publication) and may change — please verify against the current BMF publication. For the same external workplace the meal allowance applies only for the first three months.
