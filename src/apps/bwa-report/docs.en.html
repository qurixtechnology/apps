What does this app do?

BWA Report reads a DATEV BWA as a PDF and turns it into a report anyone can understand — no accounting background required. Built for startups and small businesses without a finance department. Everything runs locally in the browser; the file is not uploaded.

Import

Pick a BWA PDF by drag & drop or click. The app evaluates the BWA-relevant sheets: the Kurzfristige Erfolgsrechnung (short-term P&L) and the Summen & Salden (trial balance, used for liquidity). The Umsatzsteuer-Voranmeldung (VAT return) sheet is intentionally ignored.

The report

The report is organised into tabs: Overview (cockpit, KPIs, rating, trend), Revenue (earnings, customers, P&L), Expenses / costs (cost blocks, suppliers, expense accounts), Taxes and Liquidity and BWA (source). Printing / PDF export lays out all tabs one below the other.

  • Cockpit — the key ratios as gauge dials with green/amber/red: operating margin, break-even safety margin, liquidity runway, concentration risk, income-tax coverage (income tax only — “none due” when a loss carry-forward applies) and free liquidity — graspable at a glance.
  • At a glance — revenue, operating result, preliminary result, cash and the net outflow for taxes & pass-through money (after offsetting refunds) as KPI cards with a traffic-light rating (with a mini-trend when several months are loaded).
  • Economic situation — a P&L waterfall (from output through the cost blocks to the operating result), a plain-language assessment and a break-even estimate (with safety margin).
  • Cost structure — the largest cost blocks as bars, plus a drill-down into the largest single expense accounts (often the bulk of “other costs”).
  • Liquidity — cash, receivables, short-term liabilities, provisions, net liquidity, runway (stress test) and DSO/DPO (receivable and payable days). Plus the tax reserve (forecast): a bar splits the cash into to keep aside and freely usable, the expected current-year income tax (~30 % of taxable profit, loss carry-forward considered) with a coverage hint, and the net outflow (pass-through money + own income tax − refunds) as a liquidity buffer. Rough orientation.
  • Taxes (per BWA) — the plain actuals from the accounts, no estimates. A tax overview table shows, per tax/item — VAT, wage tax/social security, corporate and trade tax — the flow: opening (prior year) + booked this year (booked) − paid/deductible = open (balance). For income tax, the in-year advance payments (corporate/trade tax, capital-gains tax/solidarity surcharge from the P&L tax accounts) are included in “booked” and “paid”. The final row is the prior-year tax-office balance = opening VAT + opening income-tax provision − refund claims (the old balance still open with the tax office). Every figure has a tooltip deriving it from account numbers, and is traceable to its source account by clicking; the not-yet-due VAT (account 1766) is shown for information only. Plus a plausibility check (booked VAT vs. 19 % of revenue, input VAT vs. expense). The likely current-year tax burden and the liquidity buffer to keep aside live separately in the Liquidity tab (above). Rough orientation, not a tax calculation.
  • Revenue per customer — the largest customers by invoiced turnover (cumulative), with opening balance (prior year), collected payments and open balance (open = opening + invoiced − collected). Sortable columns (e.g. by open, for the receivables risk). Gross values incl. VAT from the debtor movements; only available when the BWA contains the movement columns.
  • Purchases per supplier — the largest suppliers by purchasing volume (billed by the supplier), with opening balance (prior year), payments made and open payable (open = opening + billed − paid; negative = overpayment/credit). Sortable columns. Gross values incl. input VAT; only available when the BWA contains the movement columns.
  • Customer & supplier structure — distribution of open receivables and payables across the largest names (concentration risk). Shown as a fallback when the BWA has no movement columns — otherwise the tables above cover this.
  • Year projection — the months so far extrapolated linearly to twelve months (revenue and result), as a rough orientation.
  • Tax reserve — for corporations, a rough income tax on the profit (~30 %) compared with the tax provisions set aside; detects existing loss carry-forwards and points them out. Rough orientation, not a tax calculation.
  • Rating & recommendations — an overall assessment (green / amber / red) and concrete, figure-driven suggestions.
  • Details — the full result statement and the largest expense accounts, collapsible.
  • BWA (source) — the raw values read from the BWA: the full short-term result statement and the trial balance (all accounts with opening, debit, credit, balance). Clicking a highlighted figure in the report jumps here and highlights the underlying accounts or P&L lines — so every number is traceable to its source.

Nearly every KPI carries an ⓘ icon — hover it for a short, plain-language explanation.

KPIs — briefly explained

  • Operating result: the result from the core business (output minus costs).
  • Operating margin: how much of revenue remains as operating result.
  • Personnel cost ratio: personnel costs as a share of total output.
  • Net liquidity: cash + receivables − short-term liabilities.
  • Runway: how long cash would cover the costs if no more revenue came in (a stress test, not a forecast).

Export & notes

Save as PDF / print exports a clean PDF (just the report, without the app frame). The evaluation is preliminary (current bookkeeping status) and is not tax or legal advice.
